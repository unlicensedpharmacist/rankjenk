1. THE "JENKEM CLASSIC" SMOOTHIE

SERVES 2 | 5 MIN | RANK: ★★★★★

Contains zero jenkem. Contains strawberries.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen strawberries
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1/2 cup plain or vanilla yogurt
  • A handful of ice (optional)

Directions

  1. Pour the juice and yogurt into a blender first, then add the fruit and ice.
  2. Blend for about 45 seconds, until smooth.
  3. Pour into two glasses and drink right away while it's cold.

2. GAS STATION NACHOS

SERVES 2 | 10 MIN | RANK: ★★★★☆

Inspired by the Gas Station Edition. Tastes like a good decision at 2AM.

Ingredients

  • Big handful of tortilla chips (about 6 oz)
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
  • Pickled jalapeno slices
  • Salsa and sour cream, for topping

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Spread the chips on a baking sheet and cover them with the cheese.
  3. Bake 5 to 7 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  4. Top with jalapenos, salsa, and sour cream.
  5. For full authenticity, eat standing up under a buzzing light.

3. SCENTED CANDLE CUPCAKES

MAKES 12 | 45 MIN | RANK: ★★★★★

Do not eat the candle. We cannot stress this enough.

Ingredients

  • 1 box vanilla cake mix, plus the eggs, oil, and water it calls for
  • 1 tub vanilla frosting
  • 12 birthday candles
  • Sprinkles

Directions

  1. Make the batter following the box, fill a lined 12-cup muffin tin, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 18 to 22 minutes.
  2. Let the cupcakes cool completely, then frost them.
  3. Add sprinkles and stick one candle in each cupcake.
  4. Light them, sing, and blow them out. Remove the candles before eating.

4. RANK #1 BROWNIES

MAKES 9 | 40 MIN | RANK: ★★★★★

Cut into nine pieces and give yourself first place.

Ingredients

  • 1 box brownie mix, plus the oil, eggs, and water it calls for
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • Flaky sea salt

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease an 8x8 inch pan.
  2. Mix the batter following the box, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  3. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  4. Sprinkle with salt, cool for 20 minutes, and cut into 9 squares.

5. ZERO SUGAR FLOAT

SERVES 1 | 3 MIN | RANK: ★★★☆☆

Zero sugar soda, plus ice cream. We do not talk about the ice cream.

Ingredients

  • 1 can diet cola or any zero sugar soda, chilled
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 1 tall glass and a straw

Directions

  1. Put the ice cream in the glass.
  2. Pour the soda in slowly, because it will foam up fast.
  3. Add a straw and a spoon. Enjoy.
